BIO 201B Fall 2019 Midterm, as a quiz

The Fall 2019 lab midterm, cut down to Labs 1–5 and rebuilt so you can fill it in. Answer a question, then press Check, or press Grade everything at the top. Every answer comes with an explanation, and the tags underneath show its source.

Lab 1–4 slides Your worksheets Not on the slides Matches your graded paper

Only Labs 1–5 are included: biomolecules, the microscope, the cell, mitosis & meiosis, and diffusion & osmosis. Questions from the enzyme, respiration and photosynthesis labs are left out (list below). Questions tagged Not on the slides are on a Lab 1–5 topic, but the slides don’t spell the answer out. Those use standard textbook answers. Matches your graded paper means the grader’s marks on your scanned copy confirm the answer. Written answers come with a model answer, and you mark yourself (full, half or none) so the score stays honest. Your answers are saved in this browser.

What was left out, and why
  • Multiple choice (8): the enzyme rate graph, the potato-extract enzyme (catechol oxidase), the factors that alter maltose hydrolysis, activation energy and L-amino acid dehydrogenase come from the enzyme lab. Fermentation, cellular respiration and the energy conversion in photosynthesis come from the respiration and photosynthesis labs.
  • True/False (1): “PTU binds zinc and copper”, from the enzyme lab.
  • Fill in the blanks (5 blanks): where proteins and lipids are digested, and “enzymes can be proteins or ___ / active site”.
  • Problems B, C, E and F: lipase, peroxidase, the digestive enzyme pH graphs, and trypsin. All four are enzyme experiments.
  • Not in your scan: Problem G. Also, pages 17 and 18 of the PDF are the same photo, so a page may be missing.

The question bullets on the paper aren’t numbered, and the photos were out of order, so the numbering here is new.

I. Circle the best answer

Only one answer is correct. 32 questions, 0.5 pt each (16 pts). After you check, every choice shows why it is right or wrong.

II. True or False (correct the false)

6 statements, 0.5 pt each (3 pts). The quiz grades your True/False choice. On the exam you also have to correct the false ones, so write your correction in the box and compare it with the model.

III. Fill in the blanks

10 blanks, 0.5 pt each (5 pts). Spelling is checked loosely, and common synonyms are accepted.

IV. Matching (more than 1 answer could be possible)

9 items, 0.5 pt each (4.5 pts). Tick every letter that fits.

V. Questions with short answers

14.5 pts. Part C is worth 2 pts on the paper, but its “0.25 each” only adds up to 1 pt, so each part counts 0.5 here.

VI. Problems

Problems A, D and H (22 pts). B, C, E and F were enzyme experiments, so they’re left out.
